## Tuesday The Sky: _Indoor Enthusiast_

**Tuesday The Sky**, the atmospheric, instrumental project masterminded by **Jim Matheos** (Fates Warning, OSI, North Sea Echoes, Kings of Mercia), announces its third studio album, **_Indoor Enthusiast_**.

Exploring cinematic dimensions of ambient guitar, layered electronic textures, and evocative rhythmic movements, _Indoor Enthusiast_ represents a profound evolution of the soundscapes first introduced on 2017's _Drift_ and expanded on 2021's _The Blurred Horizon_.

### Visual Identity & Album Artwork

The striking cover art and packaging for _Indoor Enthusiast_ was created by visionary artist **Simon Ward** ([Identity Media](https://identity-media.co.uk/)), capturing an otherworldly, introspective tension between isolation and boundless cosmic space.
