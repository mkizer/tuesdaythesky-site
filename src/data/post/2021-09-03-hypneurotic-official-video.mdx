### "Hypneurotic" (Official Video)

Taken from Tuesday The Sky's sophomore album, **_The Blurred Horizon_**, released via **Metal Blade Records**.

The centerpiece track **"Hypneurotic"** features a mesmerizing guest drum performance by **Gavin Harrison** (Porcupine Tree, King Crimson, The Pineapple Thief). Harrison tracked two complete, distinct drum kit performances panned left and right, producing a thrilling "double drummer" dynamic across the song's hypnotic guitar loops.
