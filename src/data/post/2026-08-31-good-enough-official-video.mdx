**'Good Enough'** is the third single released from 2026's **"How to Cast a Shadow"**.

**Ray Alder** on this track. \"**'Good Enough'** was one of the first songs written for How To Cast A Shadow. It‘s a simple yet beautiful song, and it quickly became one of my favorite songs that Jim and I had written together. While we never intended for it to be released as a single or video we decided that while we were together for a photo shoot we would also shoot a video for this. I think that we both agreed that this song was just too nice not to be shared with others."

**Ray Alder**: Vocals  
**Jim Matheos**: Guitar / Programming  
**Dennis Leeflang**: Drums
